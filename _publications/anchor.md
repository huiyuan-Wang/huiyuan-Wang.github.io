excerpt: >-
  ANCHOR uses multiple negative control outcomes to calibrate sensitivity bounds for the average treatment effect on the treated in the presence of unmeasured confounding. Under an adaptive range assumption, it obtains sharp identification bounds and uses a smooth conservative approximation with cross-fitted, Neyman-orthogonal double machine learning to enable inference. The method is illustrated by comparing the cardiovascular safety of GLP-1 receptor agonists and SGLT-2 inhibitors in patients with type 2 diabetes.
